Community Resources

Local Dayton Addiction & Community Resources

If you or someone you love is struggling, help is often closer than it feels. Below is a directory of trusted crisis lines, legal aid, shelter, food, health, and benefits resources across the Miami Valley — alongside our own treatment services.

Miami Valley Recovery is not affiliated with these organizations, but we’ve compiled them here as a convenience. If you’re not sure where to start, call us at 937.401.8672 and we’ll help point you in the right direction.

Crisis & Emergency Support

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 for free, confidential support 24/7 if you or someone you know is in emotional distress or a suicide crisis.

Call or text 988 · Available 24/7

Visit website

United Way HelpLink 211

A free, confidential 24/7 lifeline connecting callers to local resources for food, housing, utility assistance, health care, transportation, and more across Montgomery, Greene, and Preble counties.

Call 937.225.3000 or dial 211 · Available 24/7

Visit website

Crisis Care (ADAMHS Board)

Walk-in and phone crisis support for mental health and addiction emergencies, located at Elizabeth Place in Dayton.

Montgomery County Crisis Call Center · Call 833.580.2255 · Available 24/7

Visit website

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Confidential support, safety planning, and local shelter referrals for anyone experiencing domestic violence.

Call 937.222.7233 (local) or 1.800.799.7233 · Available 24/7

Visit website

Veterans Crisis Line

Confidential support for veterans and their families, staffed by responders who understand military life.

Call or text 988, then press 1 · Available 24/7

Visit website

Legal Assistance

Advocates for Basic Legal Equality (ABLE) & Legal Aid of Western Ohio

Free civil legal assistance for income-eligible individuals in the Miami Valley, covering housing, benefits, family law, and more.

130 West Second St., Ste. 700 East, Dayton · Call 937.228.8088

Visit website

Gettysburg Gateway for Men (Homefull)

A 24-hour emergency shelter for single men, operated by Homefull, offering meals, clothing, and housing case management.

Daybreak (Youth Shelter, Ages 10–24)

The Miami Valley's only 24-hour crisis hotline and emergency shelter for homeless and at-risk youth and young adults, with counseling and life-skills support.

Visit website

YWCA Dayton Domestic Violence Shelter

The domestic violence shelter and rape crisis center for Montgomery and Preble counties, with a 24/7 crisis hotline.

Call 937.222.7233 · Available 24/7
