Text Messaging Terms and Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

These terms apply to the text message program operated by Miami Valley Recovery LLC. By signing our written consent form, you agree to receive text messages from us on the terms below.

Program description

Miami Valley Recovery LLC sends appointment reminders, schedule changes, general check-in messages, and administrative messages to existing clients who have given written consent. Messages are sent from (937) 930-4672. This is not a marketing program; we do not send promotional messages.

How you join

You join by signing a paper consent form in person at our office during intake. That form authorizes SMS contact and is kept in your record. We do not add anyone from a purchased, rented, or third-party list.

Consent is optional. Refusing does not affect your treatment, and we will contact you by phone or mail instead.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Most recipients receive approximately 2 to 8 messages per month, depending on appointment schedule.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for text messages; your mobile carrier may, depending on your plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP at any time. You may also reply STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. This stops all messages immediately. You can also tell any staff member in person or by phone, or send a written request to the office.

Reply HELP or INFO for our contact information. To start receiving messages again, reply START or UNSTOP, or ask a staff member.

Not for emergencies

Do not use text messaging for urgent or emergency needs. Staff do not monitor incoming messages at all hours and may not see your message right away. In an emergency, call 911. For a mental health or substance use crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). During business hours, call the office at (937) 930-4672.

What our messages contain

Our messages never include a diagnosis, clinical information, medication names or doses, test results, the reason for an appointment, or any detail about the nature of services you receive. Text messages are not secure, and anyone with access to your phone may be able to see them. We limit message content for that reason.

Keeping your number current

Please tell us if your mobile number changes. If you do not, messages intended for you may be delivered to whoever receives that number next.

Carriers and delivery

Message delivery depends on your mobile carrier and is not guaranteed. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers may change without notice.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Text Messaging Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it is handled.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The current version is always posted on this page with its last updated date.
