This form gives Miami Valley Recovery LLC permission to contact you by text message. Texting is optional. You will receive the same quality of care whether or not you sign this form, and you can withdraw permission at any time.

2. What you are agreeing to receive

If you sign below, we may send text messages to the mobile number above for:

  • Appointment reminders, confirmations, and rescheduling
  • Changes to group or office schedules, including closures
  • General check-in messages asking you to contact the office
  • Administrative messages about paperwork or billing questions

You can expect roughly 2 to 8 messages per month, depending on how often you have appointments.

3. What we will never put in a text message

To protect your privacy, our text messages will never include:

  • Your diagnosis or any clinical information
  • The name of any medication, dose, or prescription
  • Test or lab results of any kind
  • The reason for an appointment
  • Any detail about the nature of the services you receive

If we need to discuss any of the above, we will ask you to call the office or reach you by another method you have approved.

4. Risks you should understand before signing

Text messages are not a secure or private form of communication. Please read each of the following and initial to show you understand.

Anyone with access to my phone may be able to see messages, including on the lock screen, without unlocking it.
If I share a phone or phone plan, another person may see that Miami Valley Recovery has contacted me.
Messages are stored on my phone and on the phone company's systems, and Miami Valley Recovery cannot control or delete them once sent.
If my phone is lost, stolen, or shared, the messages may be seen by someone else.
If my phone number changes, I am responsible for telling the office. Otherwise messages may go to whoever receives that number next.
Receiving a text message from Miami Valley Recovery may reveal to another person that I am connected to this organization.

5. Text messaging is not for emergencies

Do not use text messaging for urgent or emergency needs. Staff do not monitor incoming messages at all hours and may not see your message right away. In an emergency, call 911. For a mental health or substance use crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). During business hours, call the office at (937) 930-4672.

6. Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. Miami Valley Recovery does not charge you for text messages, but your phone company may.

7. How to stop receiving messages

You may stop text messages at any time, in any of these ways:

  • Reply STOP to any message we send. This takes effect immediately and automatically.
  • Tell any staff member in person or by phone, and we will record it the same day.
  • Submit a written revocation to the office (see Section 8).

Reply HELP to any message to receive our contact information. To start receiving messages again after stopping, reply START or tell a staff member.

8. Your rights: revoking this authorization

You may revoke this authorization at any time. To revoke it in writing, give a signed, dated note to any staff member or mail it to the address at the top of this form. Revocation takes effect when we receive it, except that it does not apply to messages we have already sent.

Expiration. Unless you revoke it sooner, this authorization expires one year from the date you sign it, or when you are discharged from services, whichever happens first.

Refusing to sign changes nothing about your care. Consent to receive text messages is not required to receive services. We cannot condition your treatment, payment, enrollment, or eligibility for benefits on whether you sign this form, and this form is separate from intake paperwork. If you prefer, we will contact you by phone call or mail instead.

9. Notice regarding redisclosure

This information has been disclosed to you from records protected by federal confidentiality rules (42 CFR Part 2). The federal rules prohibit you from making any further disclosure of information in this record that identifies a patient as having or having had a substance use disorder either directly, by reference to publicly available information, or through verification of such identification by another person, unless further disclosure is expressly permitted by the written consent of the individual whose information is being disclosed or as otherwise permitted by 42 CFR Part 2. A general authorization for the release of medical or other information is NOT sufficient for this purpose.
