Text Messaging Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how Miami Valley Recovery LLC handles mobile phone numbers and text message communication. It applies to the SMS program described below and supplements any other privacy notice or Notice of Privacy Practices we provide.

We do not share mobile numbers. No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party for those purposes.

What we collect

For text messaging, we collect only what is needed to reach you:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your name, so staff can identify who a message is going to
  • A record that you signed a consent form authorizing text messages, and the date
  • Any reply you send to our number, and whether you have opted out

How we use it

Your mobile number is used only to send you:

  • Appointment reminders, confirmations, and rescheduling
  • Changes to group or office schedules, including closures
  • General check-in messages asking you to contact the office
  • Administrative messages about paperwork or billing questions

We do not send promotional or marketing text messages.

What our messages never contain

To protect your privacy, our text messages never include a diagnosis, clinical information, medication names or doses, test results, the reason for an appointment, or any detail about the nature of the services you receive. When those things need to be discussed, we ask you to call the office instead.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies by individual. Most recipients receive approximately 2 to 8 messages per month, depending on how often they have appointments.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Miami Valley Recovery does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier may, depending on your plan.

How to stop messages

You can stop text messages at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any message. This takes effect immediately and automatically.
  • Tell any staff member in person or by phone, and we will record it the same day.
  • Submit a written revocation to the office at the address above.

Reply HELP to any message for our contact information. To resume messages after stopping, reply START or tell a staff member.

Consent

We text only clients who have signed a written consent form in person at our office authorizing SMS contact. We do not purchase, rent, or otherwise obtain phone numbers from third parties. Consent is not a condition of treatment — you receive the same care whether or not you agree to text messages.

Service providers

We use Twilio Inc. to deliver text messages. Twilio processes your mobile number solely to transmit our messages and is not permitted to use it for its own marketing. Delivery also requires your mobile carrier to handle the message. Beyond these providers, we do not share mobile numbers.

Security and limits of text messaging

Text messages are not a secure form of communication. Once a message reaches your phone, we cannot control who sees it, and messages may be visible on a lock screen or to anyone with access to your device. Messages are also stored by your mobile carrier. We limit what our messages contain specifically because of this.

Do not use text messaging for emergencies. Staff do not monitor incoming messages at all hours. In an emergency, call 911. For a mental health or substance use crisis, call or text 988.

Confidentiality of treatment records

Records identifying a person as receiving substance use disorder treatment are protected by federal law under 42 CFR Part 2 and, where applicable, HIPAA. Those protections apply to information we hold about you in addition to this policy. This policy does not reduce any right you have under those rules.
