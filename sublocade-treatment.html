What Sublocade is

An extended-release buprenorphine injection, given once a month in a healthcare setting.

Sublocade is an FDA-approved, once-monthly injection of extended-release buprenorphine, used to treat opioid use disorder. Unlike Suboxone or Subutex, which are taken daily under the tongue, Sublocade is a subcutaneous (under-the-skin) injection administered by a member of our clinical team — there’s no daily dosing to manage or remember.

Because it’s injected as a depot that releases medication gradually over weeks, Sublocade must be given in a healthcare setting by a provider. It is not a starting point for treatment — it’s specifically for patients who have already been stabilized on a transmucosal (under-the-tongue) buprenorphine product, such as Suboxone or Subutex, for at least a week before switching over.

Is Sublocade right for you?

Sublocade is a good fit for patients who are already stable on daily buprenorphine and want to move away from a daily routine — whether for convenience, consistency, or simply preferring fewer daily reminders of treatment. Because it requires prior stabilization, it’s not typically where treatment begins.

If you’re just starting out, we’ll likely begin with a daily Suboxone or buprenorphine regimen first. Once you’re stable, we can talk about whether transitioning to Sublocade makes sense for you.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I start treatment with Sublocade?
Not right away. Sublocade is only for patients already stabilized on a transmucosal (under-the-tongue) buprenorphine product, like Suboxone or Subutex. We’ll typically start you on a daily medication first, then discuss whether Sublocade is a good next step.
Where is Sublocade administered?
Always in a healthcare setting, by a member of our clinical team. It’s not a take-home medication — you’ll come into our clinic once a month for your injection.
Does Ohio Medicaid cover Sublocade?
In most cases, yes. We accept every Ohio Medicaid managed-care plan, and many private insurance plans cover Sublocade as well. Reach out and we’ll verify your specific benefits.
What if I miss a monthly appointment?
Let us know as soon as you can. We’ll work with you to reschedule and make sure your treatment stays on track without a lapse in coverage.
